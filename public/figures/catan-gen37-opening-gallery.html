How the sample and gallery were constructed

Seeds 3,100,000–3,100,999 from the project’s board generator. Every settlement and road is a greedy forward pass through the original Generation 37 checkpoint. Later choices are conditional on those earlier choices. All 1,000 boards remain browsable; filters highlight descriptive patterns, not proven winning strategies.

“Near its port” means a player has at least 40% of combined pips in one resource and a settlement within two geometric road edges of that resource’s 2:1 port. This distance does not guarantee an available building route. “Near-tie” means a top-two settlement logit gap below 0.1. The regression uses a fixed 800-board training / 200-board evaluation split; all four stages from one board stay together.
